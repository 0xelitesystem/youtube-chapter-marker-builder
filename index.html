Chapter Marker Builder

// Validate and format YouTube chapter timestamps

YouTube renders video chapters when the description contains properly formatted timestamps that meet specific rules. This tool validates your chapters against those rules and outputs a YouTube-formatted block ready to paste into your description.

Chapter input

Paste a chapter block in any common format. The tool parses timestamps and titles separated by whitespace or punctuation.

Validation

    Formatted output

    Paste this into your video description. The first chapter must be at 0:00 for YouTube to render the chapter UI.

    
    
    
      
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    YouTube chapter rules
    
    
    
      
    First chapter
    Must start at 00:00
    
      
    Minimum count
    At least 3 chapters
    
      
    Minimum gap
    Each chapter at least 10 seconds long
    
      
    Order
    Timestamps must be in ascending order
    
      
    Format
    One chapter per line: TIMESTAMP TITLE (e.g. 0:00 Intro)
    
      
    Timestamp format
    MM:SS or H:MM:SS or HH:MM:SS
